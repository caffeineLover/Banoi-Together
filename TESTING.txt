Failure classification
acceptance detection: no button log, unsupported mode, or no scoped commit.
quest identification: callback was reached but its native name was unavailable.
title resolution: callback/ID succeeded but the explicit title was unavailable
or differs from what the screen displayed.
chat send: no normal local-player context, sender failure/crash, or no ordinary
message emitted. A "sender returned" log is not proof that it emitted a packet.
remote delivery: stock chat appears locally but the peer receives nothing or
the wrong sender attribution. Confirm manually typed chat first.

If mode 0 is used, the experiment deliberately sends nothing: its choice-write
routine was traced, but a definitive subsequent commit was not established.
The next step is read-only runtime tracing of that commit, not changing quest
state or inventing another networking path.
